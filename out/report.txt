10. VERDICT ON THE HYPOTHESIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hypothesis: 'Women vote for both male and female authors;
men vote more heavily for male authors.'

  Male voters: 70.2% of ballots to male authors (29.8% to female).
  Female voters: 49.7% of ballots to male authors (50.3% to female).
  Two-proportion z = 6.24, p < 0.0001 — strongly supported.

  Women vote ~evenly between male and female authors.
  Men vote ~2.4x more often for male authors than female authors.
  The asymmetry is in the men, not the women.
